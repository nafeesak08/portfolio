CASE 03 — SYSTEMIC RESEARCH

Design research for dignity: inside and beyond custody

A Double Diamond research project surfacing systemic barriers facing incarcerated Indigenous women, mapping digital opportunities for self-advocacy, healing access, and reintegration support.

Research-driven concept exploration · not yet validated with users

ROLEUX Research
METHODDouble Diamond
STATUSResearch complete · concepts not yet co-designed with community

Overview

Indigenous women are dramatically overrepresented in Canadian correctional systems, and the systems meant to support them — classification, cultural and healing programs, reintegration planning — are often disconnected from one another and hard to navigate. This project set out to understand those systemic barriers firsthand and translate them into concrete opportunities for digital support, grounded in interviews, literature review, and a culturally-adapted research process.

A culturally grounded research process

The Double Diamond framework was adapted throughout to stay accountable to lived experience rather than assumptions — pairing each phase with community-informed research methods.

What we heard

Interviews and lived-experience accounts surfaced recurring frustrations with opacity, fragmentation, and inconsistent access to cultural support.

"It's hard to understand why I qualify for some programs but not others. I wish the rules were clearer."
"Access to cultural healing shouldn't feel optional — it's something people need to stay grounded."
"I wish there was one place that explained what programs are available and how to apply."
"It would help to know what supports I can access after release before I actually leave."
"Connecting with elders or cultural mentors should be easier to find and request."
"I want clearer information about what steps I need to take to prepare for reintegration."
"Sometimes information about programs or supports feels scattered or hard to access."

Persona

A representative persona grounded the research in a single, continuous story rather than a list of disconnected pain points.

From insight to opportunity

Three recurring insights were translated directly into the digital opportunity areas explored in the concepts below.

1. Eligibility opacity

INSIGHTWomen struggle to understand why they qualify for programs.
↓
OPPORTUNITYDesign a tool that shows program eligibility and qualification steps.

2. Cultural healing is essential

INSIGHTParticipants view cultural practices as central to wellbeing.
↓
OPPORTUNITYDevelop a virtual healing circle where women can connect with elders and mentors.

3. Reintegration systems are fragmented

INSIGHTSupport systems lack a clear starting point.
↓
OPPORTUNITYBuild a reintegration app/portal that shows local services and support.

Conceptual experience flow

Accessing healing and reintegration support, mapped end to end:

  1. 01

    Discover support

    Learn about the platform through correctional programs or a community worker.

  2. 02

    Create a secure profile

    Select cultural preferences and indicate reintegration stage.

  3. 03

    Explore healing resources

    Browse programs, see eligibility info, and access storytelling modules.

  4. 04

    Connect with elders & community

    Message elders or cultural mentors directly, and see who's available to connect with this week.

  5. 05

    Reintegration planning

    View local services and track eligibility for support programs.

  6. 06

    Ongoing support

    Access peer networks and track goals post-release.

Concept sketches

Concept sketch · based on research findings · not yet validated with users

Three touchpoints translate the opportunity areas above into a first, rough shape — a starting point for future co-design, not a finished product.

Interactive concept prototype

Next steps

These four opportunity concepts were developed directly from the research but have not yet been co-designed or tested with Indigenous women or community partners — the research itself identifies that as the necessary next step before any of this moves toward a real product.
