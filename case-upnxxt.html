CASE 02 — AI & TRUST

Upnxxt: helping kids start with AI confidently

A guided onboarding flow and website layout that helps educators and families understand the value of early AI learning and reach the first meaningful action faster.

ROLEUX Designer
PLATFORMWeb (parent & educator dashboards)
TOOLSFigma

Overview

Parents and educators want kids learning with AI — but they need to trust the platform before they'll hand over that first sign-up. The work here focused on the onboarding flow and site layout that gets a skeptical adult to a confident "yes."

What people told us

Interviews with parents and educators surfaced a shared theme: enthusiasm about AI's potential, paired with real questions about safety, transparency, and time.

Looking at the landscape

Before designing, we looked at how comparable platforms introduce themselves to parents, teachers, and kids — where they lead with trust, and where they lead with excitement.

Personas

The original persona images were AI-generated and contained garbled, cut-off text that didn't hold up on close reading — so they've been rebuilt here as real, coded persona cards in the site's own design system.

SM

Sarah Malik

37 · MARKETING MANAGER

A busy working mom of two, ages 7 and 10. She values educational technology that supports creativity and learning but is wary of screen time and online safety, and appreciates platforms that give her visibility and control over what her kids do online.

WANTS
  • Safe, age-appropriate AI activities for her kids
  • A dashboard to monitor progress and activity
  • Easy onboarding without heavy tech setup
  • Clear guidance on how AI supports learning outcomes
PAIN POINTS
  • Concerns about privacy and data security
  • Hard-to-understand tech jargon from platforms
  • Overwhelmed by too many apps to manage
DC

Daniel Chen

42 · ELEMENTARY TEACHER, STEM SPECIALIST

Daniel has 15 years of teaching experience and is enthusiastic about integrating new technology in the classroom. He values AI as a tool for fostering problem-solving and creativity, and looks for platforms that are transparent, collaborative, and aligned with curriculum standards.

WANTS
  • AI activities mapped to learning goals and curriculum
  • Quick setup for classroom use without losing teaching time
  • Safe, moderated environments for kids
  • Strong collaboration tools to update and inform parents
PAIN POINTS
  • Limited time to explain tech use to parents
  • Concerns over student safety and data policies
  • Difficulty balancing new tools with existing lesson plans

Note: the "overwhelmed by too many apps" pain point was cut off in the original research artifact and completed as "...to manage" for this rebuild — flagged for confirmation.
